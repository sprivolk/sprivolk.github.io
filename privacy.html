What we store

  • Google account identifier. When you sign in with Google, the game receives a sign-in token and our server keeps only Google's numeric account identifier (the "sub" value) to recognise your account. We do not request or store your email address, name, photo, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Device key. The game creates a cryptographic key on your device; our server keeps only its public part to let that device sign in.
  • Game data. Your nickname, avatar, friends list, messages to other players, inventory, match history and statistics.
  • Technical reports (can be turned off in Settings › Game): frame rate, ping and packet loss of your matches, errors the game hit, device model, graphics chip and OS version. These reports are not linked to your account or nickname and are deleted after 30 days.
  • Network addresses. Your IP address is used to connect you to servers and other players and is not kept with your account.

Why

Only to run the game: to sign you in, keep your account and items, connect you to matches and friends, and fix bugs and performance problems. We do not sell data, show ads or share data for advertising.

Third parties

Sign-in uses Google. Online connections between players may go through Epic Online Services. Their own privacy policies apply to the data they process.

Guests

If you play as a guest, the game does not create an account on our server.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all data linked to it through this site's issue page or through the testers' community where you got the game. You can also remove the game's access in your Google account settings (Security › Third-party apps).

Children

The game is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Кратко по-русски

При входе через Google сервер игры хранит только числовой идентификатор аккаунта Google — без почты, имени и фото. Ещё хранятся открытая часть ключа вашего устройства, ник, аватар, друзья, сообщения, инвентарь и история матчей. Технические отчёты (FPS, пинг, ошибки, модель устройства) не связаны с аккаунтом, хранятся 30 дней и отключаются в настройках. Данные не продаются и не используются для рекламы. Удалить аккаунт можно по запросу через страницу issues этого сайта или в сообществе тестеров.
